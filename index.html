The Client Brief

Northstar SaaS Co.

Your client is a B2B SaaS company with 3,200 customers. They want an AI agent that reads their customer data and helps their Customer Success team answer questions like: "Which accounts are at risk of churning?" and "Who hasn't logged in for 30 days?"

Client Situation

Northstar's Customer Success (CS) team of 12 currently pulls weekly reports manually from two systems: their HubSpot CRM (account records, contact info, deal history) and a Google Sheet maintained by the ops team (manual churn scores, NPS survey results, support ticket counts, last-login dates from their product database export).

The CS team wants an agent they can ask natural-language questions to. The agent should read both sources, find relevant accounts, and surface insights without the CS manager having to write a single formula.

CRM (HubSpot)
  • 👤 Contact: name, email, phone, title
  • 🏢 Company: name, industry, size, ARR
  • 💼 Deal: stage, close date, MRR, owner
  • 🗒 Notes: CS rep call notes (free text)
  • 📅 Activity: email opens, meeting history
  • 🔐 Billing: payment method type, status
Google Sheet (Ops Export)
  • 🕐 Last login date (from product DB)
  • 📊 Monthly active users (MAU) count
  • 🎯 NPS score (0–10, last survey)
  • 🎫 Support tickets opened (last 90d)
  • ⚠️ Churn risk score (manual, 1–5)
  • 📝 CS notes (free text, ops additions)
⚠️ What makes this hard: The data contains both operational metrics (fine to read) and personal information (needs justification). Some fields the agent could access have no legitimate purpose for churn analysis — but they're in the same source. Your job is to design the integration precisely, not permissively.
